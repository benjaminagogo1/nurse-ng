How activity is counted {{ icon('chevron-down', 16) }}

Each signed-in account counts once per Nigerian calendar day (WAT, UTC+1), across visits and devices. The same account counts once in each 7- or 30-day total.

Page visits and recent interaction on a visible page record activity. Automatic updates, hidden tabs and idle pages do not keep someone active. “Last 5 minutes” is a recent-use estimate, not a guarantee that someone is online.

Staff accounts are excluded unless you select Include staff. Account type controls the summary cards and chart; name and activity filters narrow the user list. Nurse account type is self-selected and does not establish professional verification.

History begins with recorded use after this feature was enabled. The first day may be partial. Deleted accounts and their activity are removed from these counts. Activity is visible to staff; it is not shown on public profiles.
