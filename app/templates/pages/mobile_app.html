Install on this device

Add Nurse.ng to your home screen or computer’s apps for quick access. Sign in while connected and save the work you want to use offline.

Choose your device below for installation steps.

Installing on a computer
  1. Open Nurse.ng in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Use the install icon in the address bar or open the browser menu and choose Install Nurse.ng or Install this site as an app.
  3. Confirm Install, then open Nurse.ng from your apps.

Android

Open Nurse.ng from your phone’s home screen.

Install on Android {{ icon('arrow', 16) }}
  1. Open Nurse.ng in Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap Install Nurse.ng above if it appears. You can also open Chrome’s three-dot menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm Install or Add, then open Nurse.ng from your home screen.

iPhone

Add Nurse.ng using Safari’s Share menu.

Install on iPhone {{ icon('arrow', 16) }}
  1. Open Nurse.ng in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen. Scroll through the Share menu if needed.
  3. If Open as Web App appears, turn it on. Tap Add, then open Nurse.ng from your home screen.
