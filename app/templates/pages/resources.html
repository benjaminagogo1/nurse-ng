Good to know

Who can join Nurse.ng?
Nurses at every stage, healthcare employers, and partners are welcome. Choose your account type when you register. Nurses can build profiles and apply for jobs; employers can publish opportunities and review applications.
Who can see my profile?
Your profile starts private. Use sharing controls to choose which fields can be public. Publishing your profile adds it to nurse discovery; making it private removes it again. Document uploads are never shown on your public profile.
What does “Document reviewed” mean?
A reviewer has inspected the submitted licence or certificate and identity document and matched the names. It does not confirm the licence against the NMCN register, certify authenticity, or assess clinical competence. The official register remains the source for registration checks. Learn about document review.
How do job applications work?
Open a role, introduce yourself, and agree to share the contact details shown on the application form with that employer. Track the application in your workspace. Your private profile fields are not included automatically.
How do I start a conversation?
Choose a unique community name in community settings, then join a public discussion. To send a private message, first make a connection that the other person accepts.
Where can I check official registration information?
Visit the Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria {{ icon('external', 14) }}(opens in a new tab) for official information about registration and professional practice.
